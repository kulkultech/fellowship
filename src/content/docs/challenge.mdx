Welcome to the **KulKul Fellowship Onboarding Challenge**. To join the fellowship, you must complete the following 10 steps, which are designed to transition you into an AI-native developer workflow.

This challenge is a practical screening process. We focus on your **code understanding**, **prompting strategy**, and **collaboration etiquette** rather than manual syntax memorization.

---

## 🏃‍♂️ The 10 Steps Index

Below is the index of the 10-Step Challenge. Click any step to open its detailed, step-by-step text guide:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="1. Community Setup" icon="peoples">
    Connect with the team on GitHub, join Telegram & Discord chat.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 1 Guide](/challenge/step-1/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="2. OS Environment Setup" icon="setting">
    Install terminal environments, NVM, Node.js, and configure Git SSH.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 2 Guide](/challenge/step-2/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="3. Configure Coding Agents" icon="laptop">
    Install Cursor or Antigravity IDE and configure Privacy Modes.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 3 Guide](/challenge/step-3/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="4. Select Helper Stack" icon="open-book">
    Understand helper options (ChatGPT, OpenCode, Antigravity) and API costs.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 4 Guide](/challenge/step-4/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="5. Fork & Clone Sandbox" icon="rocket">
    Fork the repo and clone locally. Check for AGENTS.md & DESIGN.md.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 5 Guide](/challenge/step-5/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="6. Sandbox Architecture" icon="structure">
    Understand the Vite (React) + Supabase (PostgreSQL) architecture.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 6 Guide](/challenge/step-6/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="7. Solve Sandbox Issue" icon="document">
    Check out a topic branch and solve an open bug ticket in the sandbox.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 7 Guide](/challenge/step-7/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="8. Prompt Automated Testing" icon="approve-list">
    Prompt AI to generate unit tests and run Vitest locally.
    <br/><br/>
    [Read Step 8 Guide](/challenge/step-8/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="9. Deploy Your Application" icon="external">
    Publish your sandbox frontend to Vercel and link a live Supabase DB.

    [Read Step 9 Guide](/challenge/step-9/)

  <Card title="10. Submit & Review" icon="pencil">

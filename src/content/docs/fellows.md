Welcome to the **KulKul Fellowship Community**! 

This page serves as a directory of all the developers who have successfully completed the onboarding challenge and joined the fellowship.

---

## 👥 Meet Our Fellows

Our fellows come from various backgrounds, working together in a peer-to-peer environment to learn, collaborate, and build open-source software.

Look at the sidebar under **Our Fellows** to view individual profiles!

---

## ✍️ How to Add Yourself

As part of the [10-Step Challenge](/challenge/), you are required to add your profile here.

1. Create a new file in `src/content/docs/fellows/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME.md`.
2. Add your name, bio, interests, and GitHub profile.
3. Commit (without GPG signing!) and open a Pull Request.

Once your PR is merged, your profile will automatically appear in the sidebar navigation!

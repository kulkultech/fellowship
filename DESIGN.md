# KulKul.tech Design Principles

This repository follows the design principles and specifications outlined in the GetDesign framework.

Learn more at: **[getdesign.md](https://getdesign.md)**

---

## 🎨 Aesthetic Intent
*   **Vibe:** Premium, modern, dark-first, and highly engaging.
*   **Visual Elements:** Glassmorphism, tailored HSL color palettes, responsive cards, and clean typography.
*   **Aims:** Make technical documentation look state-of-the-art and approachable for tech-newbies.
